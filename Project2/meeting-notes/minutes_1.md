## Discussion and decisions
I reviewed the Student Task Tracker idea from Project 1. The target users are college students managing assignments across multiple courses. First-time users need simple assignment entry. Returning users need a clear list of deadlines and unfinished tasks.

I selected three screens: a dashboard, an Add Assignment form, and an Edit Assignment form. The dashboard will show titles, courses, due dates, status, and filters. Both forms will use the same assignment fields. Editing will also allow completion and deletion.

I assigned all design, documentation, review, and submission tasks to myself. The instructor remains the ScrumMaster. I excluded accounts, shared assignments, and automatic notifications from this prototype stage. I selected static screen images as the deliverable format.
